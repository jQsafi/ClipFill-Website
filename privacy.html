Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 • Extension Version: 1.0.0
🔒 Summary Pledge: ClipFill operates on a 100% local-first privacy model. All copied snippets, form templates, and personal profile information are stored exclusively in your browser local storage (chrome.storage.local). Zero data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.

1. Information Collection & Storage

ClipFill collects and stores information solely to provide clipboard management and form filling functionality:

  • Copied Text Snippets: Text that you explicitly copy (or auto-captured when enabled) is saved to your sidebar history.
  • Form Profile Data: Personal profile fields (such as Name, Email, Phone, Address) that you enter or edit in the sidebar.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as Auto-Capture toggle state, category tags, and pinned status.

2. Where Data is Stored

All stored data remains strictly on your local device inside the web browser's isolated local storage area (chrome.storage.local). Your data is never sent to any external server, cloud platform, database, or third-party service.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data. Because ClipFill has zero remote servers, no third parties have access to your data under any circumstances.

4. Browser Permissions Usage

ClipFill requests only the necessary permissions required to operate:

  • sidePanel: Renders the ClipFill sidebar interface alongside active web pages.
  • storage: Stores your clipboard snippets and form profile data locally.
  • contextMenus: Enables the right-click "Save selection to ClipFill" menu item.
  • tabs & scripting: Allows inspecting active page inputs and inserting selected text when you click a snippet or trigger form auto-fill.
  • clipboardRead & clipboardWrite: Enables reading copied text for snippet history and copying snippets back to your system clipboard.
  • Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Enables form auto-filling across web pages you choose to interact with.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data at all times:

  • You can turn off Auto-Capture at any time using the toggle switch in the sidebar header.
  • You can delete individual snippets or clear history directly from the sidebar UI.
  • Uninstalling the extension instantly and permanently deletes all stored extension data from your browser.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this webpage.
